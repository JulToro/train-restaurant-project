## Measurements

Voltage/current at supply and locomotive, speed, wheel slip, drawbar pull, motor/gearbox/rail temperature, derailments, stop distance, spill mass, ambient temperature, mass distribution and interventions.

## Method

1. Calibrate instruments; inspect/lubricate per manual.
2. Establish empty baseline and repeatability.
3. Increase one variable at a time.
4. Repeat both directions; randomize where practical.
5. Cool/inspect between overload trials.
6. Photograph setup and record rail condition.

## Candidate stop criteria

Visible damage, persistent derailment, uncontrolled speed, electrical protection trip, smoke/odor, abnormal noise, runaway temperature trend or unsafe condition. No numeric thermal limit is invented; request manufacturer limit and define conservative review before testing.

## Result gate

Pass/fail thresholds for speed, reliability and temperature must be approved before each formal run. Raw data remains attached to experiment record.
